Beyond Research

I believe a good life is more than work. Outside the lab, I spend my time reading, teaching, and staying active.

Reading

I enjoy classic novels and philosophy. Many of my favorite books are about human dignity and freedom: Les Misérables, David Copperfield, Jane Eyre, War and Peace, and 1984.

In philosophy, I am drawn to Kant's idea that every person should be treated as an end, never merely as a means. I first explored these ideas in depth through Michael Sandel's Harvard course Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? I also enjoy reading existentialist thinkers such as Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, and Sartre.

Teaching & Mentoring

I enjoy teaching. Besides serving as a TA for PHYS 214 (Quantum Mechanics) at ZJUI, I tutor students in my spare time. Explaining an idea to someone else is often how I find out whether I really understand it.

I believe research is better when people from different backgrounds can take part, and I am always happy to help students who are just getting started.

Sports

I love sports, especially running, basketball, cycling, swimming, and strength training. In college, I won third place at a Northeastern University track meet. My best times are 2:20 for 800 m and 3:10 for 1000 m.
